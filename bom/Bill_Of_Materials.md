### Key Manufacturing & Assembly Tolerances
* **Rotor Shaft Bearing Journal:** ISO **h6** tolerance ($25.000\text{ mm} / 24.987\text{ mm}$) for zero play bearing fitment.
* **Hub Mounting Flange Hole Centers:** True position $\varnothing 0.15\text{ mm}$ to ensure uniform blade cutting radius.
* **V-Belt Pulley Alignment:** Parallelism within $< 0.5\text{ mm}$ using the custom locating jig.
* **Corrosion Protection:** Zinc chromate yellow primer followed by high-durability red powder coating.
